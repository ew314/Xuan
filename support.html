Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no server. Everything you create stays on your device.

How do I restore a subscription?

Open Me → Store and tap Restore Purchases. Subscriptions are tied to the Apple ID you bought them with, so the same Apple ID restores them on any device. Credit packs are consumable and cannot be restored.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your device open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, choose Xuan Divination, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period stays active until it ends.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by us — the developer cannot issue or deny one. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, or open Settings → Apple ID → Media & Purchases → Purchase History → Report a Problem. Apple decides each request under its Media Services Terms and Conditions.

What are credits?

Credits are the in-app currency for individual readings. You can buy a credit pack (50 / 180 / 450 credits) or unlock a single reading with a one-time purchase. A Xuan Premium subscription does not use credits at all — while it is active, every reading is included. Credit packs do not expire, but they are stored on the device where you bought them: deleting the app removes any unused credits and they cannot be restored. Buy only what you need.

I deleted the app or switched phones — did I lose my purchase?

Your subscription is safe: it belongs to your Apple ID, deleting the app does not cancel it, and Restore Purchases brings it back. Unused credits are not — they are stored on the device only, so they are removed together with the app. Saved readings are device-only as well.

Where are my saved readings?

They are stored locally on your device. Removing the app removes them, so export the readings you want to keep (each reading can be saved as a long image to Photos).

How do I delete all my data?

Open Me → Settings → Erase Personal Data. This clears your birth profile, saved partner details, the purchase history list and your preferences. Your credit balance and subscription are kept. It cannot be undone.

The content feels like advice. Is it?

No. Xuan Divination is provided for entertainment and cultural interest only. It is not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice, and it must not be used to make important decisions.

Contact us

The fastest way to reach us is the public issue tracker: github.com/ew314/Xuan/issues. We usually reply within two business days.

When reporting a problem, please tell us your iOS version, your device model, and which screen the problem happened on. Do not include personal information.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no server. Everything you create stays on your device.

How do I restore a subscription?

Open Me → Store and tap Restore Purchases. Subscriptions are tied to the Apple ID you bought them with, so the same Apple ID on any device will restore them. Credit packs are consumable and cannot be restored — this is normal for consumable purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us. On your device open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, choose Xuan Divination, and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; the current period stays active until it ends.

What are credits?

Credits are the in-app currency used for individual readings. You can buy a credit pack, take a monthly subscription that grants credits every month, or unlock a single reading with a one-time purchase. Credits never expire; the monthly allowance from a subscription resets at each renewal.

Where are my saved readings?

They are stored locally on your device. Removing the app removes them, so export the readings you want to keep (each reading can be saved as a long image to Photos).

How do I delete all my data?

Open Me → Data → Erase All Data. This clears every locally stored reading, balance and setting. It cannot be undone.

The content feels like advice. Is it?

No. Xuan Divination is provided for entertainment and cultural interest only. It is not medical, legal, financial or psychological advice, and it must not be used to make important decisions.
